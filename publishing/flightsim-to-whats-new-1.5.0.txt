🚀 [1.5.0] - 2026-08-28
✨ Added

    The Aircraft menu now displays a real freely licensed photo beside every
    supported aircraft family. Additional aircraft found in the Community
    folder use their own local thumbnail when available, and every photo can
    be clicked to open a larger preview inside NaviXav.

    ChartFox is now integrated into NaviXav as an optional chart source. Link
    your VATSIM account once from Settings, then access ChartFox AIRAC charts
    directly for your departure and arrival airports. The Charts menu clearly
    indicates when an account is required and provides a shortcut to the
    connection setting. If a chart provider prevents embedded viewing,
    NaviXav offers to open the chart on ChartFox or use the official national
    catalogue when one is available.

    Flight tracking now calculates the Top of Climb and Top of Descent from
    the flight data. TOC and TOD appear on the map as distinct fixes with their
    own colours.

🛠️ Fixed

    Opening a PDF in Charts no longer pushes the other airport to the bottom
    of the page. Departure and Arrival remain side by side on wide screens,
    while the unopened airport stays easy to reach in compact windows.

    SIDs now display their complete path, fixes and published constraints when
    a procedure serves several runway ends. STARs also recover the final part
    specific to the landing runway. NaviXav refreshes the simulator navigation
    database automatically at the next start.

    Aircraft photos are no longer hidden behind the ICAO type tile in the
    current-aircraft card or the installed-aircraft inventory.

    The ChartFox "simulation only" notice and the weather briefing now follow
    the selected interface language. ChartFox pages that forbid embedding also
    show a clear explanation instead of a blank area.

    METAR decoding now reports TS, VCTS and VCSH groups even when no
    precipitation is observed, and no longer mistakes the "PO" in "TEMPO" for
    dust whirls.

    CartoDB Positron and Dark Matter were removed after their free tiles began
    displaying an API-key watermark. Invalid saved choices now fall back to
    OpenStreetMap Standard automatically; OpenTopoMap remains available.
