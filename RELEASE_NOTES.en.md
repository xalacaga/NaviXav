# NaviXav 1.9.0

Released on 2026-11-08.

## Added

- User guide: a question-mark icon at the top of the settings opens a guide to every menu, module and setting, with step-by-step setup and troubleshooting, in all eight interface languages.
- ATC radio ambience: optional English radio exchanges play in the background according to the flight phase — ground, tower, approach or centre — with a VHF radio effect, several controller and crew voices, fully offline; they pause during NaviXav callouts and the volume is adjustable in the settings.
- Live weather map: optional RainViewer precipitation radar, active SIGMETs and departure/arrival METAR wind barbs are drawn directly over the flight map, with locally remembered layer switches, visible timestamps and source attribution.
